The Polaris brand mark: a four-point north star cut into an offset "block" square, plus the expanded uppercase wordmark.
```jsx
<Logo size={28} />
<Logo variant="mark" size={40} />
```
- The block takes the current module accent; keep teal (`data-module="polaris"`) on landing/login.
- Static files: assets/logo-mark.svg, assets/app-icon.svg, assets/app-icon-512.png.
